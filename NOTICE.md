# Notice on third-party documents

`corpus/documents/` and `corpus/changelogs/` contain safety frameworks, companion documents and revision accounts published by Amazon, Anthropic, Cohere, G42, Google DeepMind, Magic, Meta, Microsoft, Naver, NVIDIA, OpenAI and xAI. These documents are the property of their respective publishers. They are reproduced here unmodified and in full, together with a SHA-256 hash of each file as retrieved, for one purpose: so that a reader can verify the coding in this repository against the exact text it was coded from, including for versions the publisher has since replaced or withdrawn.

No claim of ownership is made over these documents, and the CC BY 4.0 licence covering this repository's author-created materials does not extend to them. Each file's origin, retrieval URL and retrieval date are recorded in `corpus/manifest.csv`.

If you are a rights holder and wish a document removed, open an issue or write to yiven.zhu@oii.ox.ac.uk. The manifest row, hash and retrieval record will be retained so that the analysis remains verifiable, and the file itself will be removed.
