# Methods note for the paper (coding procedure, stated accurately)

Use this as the basis for the methods paragraph. Every sentence here is true of what was done.

**Corpus.** 52 manifest rows: 43 labelled framework versions and silent same-label variants across 12 providers, plus 9 companion documents. Every retrieved file is SHA-256 hashed with retrieval provenance (34 from providers, 14 from the Internet Archive, 1 manual download). Nine SaferAI quotation checks passed.

**Tracing and first-pass coding.** For each of twelve version pairs, commitments in the earlier version were listed from a recall-oriented sentence extraction, filtered by the codebook's inclusion rules, and traced into the later version; additions were detected in the reverse direction. First-pass coding of outcome, materiality, and announcement status was performed with LLM assistance following the frozen codebook (v0.2), producing 710 rows with verbatim text on both sides, a stated rationale, and a confidence grade. Every quoted field was mechanically verified against the corpus text (2,130 checks; 23 fuzzy matches at page breaks, flagged).

**Adjudication.** The first author adjudicated 244 rows individually: all 50 units in the second-coder sample, every row affected by a post-first-pass codebook decision (present-tense rule, enumeration rule, MIX flag, partial-announcement code, provider-account definition, companion-document rule), and every low-confidence material row. Adjudication used the paired passages, the first-pass rationale, and the provider's revision account, and applied the codebook, not the first-pass code. The remaining 466 rows were accepted after a random spot-check of 45 (one change, 2%). Adjudication changed 4 outcome codes and 54 announcement codes; nearly all announcement changes were reclassifications from SIL or ANN to the new partial-announcement code ANN-P.

**Agreement.** A second coder, blind to all first-pass and adjudicated codes, independently coded a stratified sample of 50 units (five per traced pair with a revision account, ten from xAI) from the paired passages, the codebook and the revision accounts, recording materiality, outcome, and announcement status. Krippendorff's α (nominal, bootstrap CI) is reported for each decision separately. [Fill in after Emilio returns.]

**Metrics.** Silent revision rate = material changes not identifiable in the provider's revision account / all material changes, computed over the eight pairs with a revision account. Two thresholds: strict (class-level mention without direction counts as silent) and lenient (it counts as announced). xAI's four pairs have no revision account and are reported separately by direction only. Silent same-label re-uploads (nine) are reported as a separate table.

**Limitations to state.** (1) Coding is interpretive; α bounds the reliability. (2) Naver's 2024 version exists only as an English summary web page, so NAV-1 over-counts additions. (3) Anthropic's Frontier Compliance Framework versions contemporaneous with RSP v3.0 are no longer publicly retained; relocation was checked against FCF v2 (July 2026). (4) Internet Archive rate limiting prevented some neighbour-capture checks; "no later version" findings rest on specific-URL checks. (5) The rule that a class-level changelog line with a stated direction counts as announced is a judgement call; ANN-P and the two thresholds make its effect visible.
